HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Fight through 3 levels of robot enemies — City Defense, Industrial Assault, and the Robot Stronghold boss fight. Clear each level's robots, then reach the glowing extraction beacon (Level 3 goes straight to the boss instead). A continuous timer tracks your full run, and your score is damage dealt minus damage taken — clearing the whole game multiplies your final score 5x.

Tips

Flight fuel (bottom-left bar) is separate from weapon energy — you can shoot freely without grounding yourself, but fuel only refills while you're on the ground. Watch the radar (top-right) for enemy positions and the extraction beacon. Every 4 kills heals you a little, and clearing a level fully restores your health.
